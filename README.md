# Echoes of the Lost Planet — Multi-Level Edition

## Objective
Restore the energy system of the Lost Planet across **5 increasingly difficult levels**.

## Levels
1. **Arrival** — 3 crystals, 1 meteor, 4 obstacles, 60 seconds
2. **Crystal Canyon** — 4 crystals, 2 meteors, 6 obstacles, 58 seconds
3. **Storm Valley** — 5 crystals, 3 meteors, 8 obstacles, 55 seconds
4. **Ancient Ruins** — 6 crystals, 4 meteors, 10 obstacles, 52 seconds, 2 health
5. **Core of the Planet** — 7 crystals, 5 faster meteors, 12 obstacles, 50 seconds, 2 health

Each level:
1. Collect all crystals.
2. Solve the Power Core memory puzzle. The sequence grows from 4 inputs at Level 1 to 8 inputs at Level 5. The sequence is hidden after a short reveal; a wrong input scraps the sequence and starts a brand-new (guaranteed different) one from the memorize phase, while completing it correctly restores +10 seconds of oxygen (shown as a small auto-dismissing notice, not a blocking dialog).
3. Oxygen continues counting down during the puzzle, so taking too long increases the risk.
4. Reach the spaceship.
5. Advance to the next level.

## Controls
- Desktop: Arrow keys or WASD to move, Space to dodge (short dash + brief invulnerability, on a cooldown), R to instantly restart the current stage
- Mobile: On-screen directional buttons plus a Dodge button

## Death & restart
Dying (health hitting 0, or oxygen running out) shows a brief failure notice and then automatically restarts the current stage — no button to click. The R key does the same thing on demand, any time.
